summary.json is the primary small integration artifact. first / last contain chosen observations; years uses actual seconds / 365.25 days. scenarios has twelve deterministic combinations: c=0/.003/.006 and independent de/dx=0/.2. gross_factor is BEFORE entry/exit cash cost. invested_after_entry_cost = 2500*(1-c); exit_before_cost = invested_after_entry_cost*gross_factor; terminal_deployed = exit_before_cost*(1-c); terminal_total = 2500+terminal_deployed. Both CAGR denominators include the entire original cash budget, including costs. Gas remains zero/unknown, not assumed free in reality. Initial coins use the pool's dollar-valued proportional composition, not forced 50:50 USD.

nav-panel.LOCAL-ONLY.csv is a locally derived full-resolution panel, NOT a publishable repository artifact. It includes balances and source-derived data. Fields: timestamp/utc/block; price_day as UTC opening epoch of prior calendar day, price_available_at as its close boundary; USD closes; pool balances/supply/value; linear per-normalized-LP token quantities and NAV for zero and limiting maximum pending exit; fixed_lp_2500_zeroentry_nocost and zero/max-exit capital paths. To mark an initial budget after entry cost, multiply the no-cost capital path by (1-c). Only an actual final modeled exit adds the final (1-c), not every snapshot.

Per-1-LP quantities are linear accounting units, not feasible burning quantities: 1 normalized LP exceeds this pool's observed supply. A physical holder uses its tiny minted_lp_approx. max is division by 1.2, a conservative continuous limiting valuation, not exact raw integer protocol mint. Floats do not support exact burn floors/dust proof.

Each snapshot denominator includes all past observed supply changes. No repeated annual fee subtraction or fixed ownership fraction. Unit-share entitlement is frozen-share accounting; NAV is not pure swap fee or realized receipts.

Critical distinction: de=.2 mechanical mint sensitivity changes the protocol supply by nearly 20%, independently of user alpha. It is NOT a small price-taker perturbation and its unadjusted historical continuation is structurally unverified. Do not rank it as achievable performance or a genuine return upper bound. Even de=0 is conditional on no endpoint pending fee and unchanged counterfactual dynamics. Single-state max exit bound cannot establish a multi-year return bound.
