# C51 / r19: TSA count-family supplement, source-gated draft

## Decision
Keep this single candidate as a **draft evidence note supplementing quant-012**, not a new entry merely to count work, not a new mechanism, and not a 待核 submission. The frozen protocol admits **0 of 40 holdout dates**, because its prerequisite defining the target statistical day's timezone/day boundary was not established. Do not spend the 99-trade request budget on ineligible windows. This rejects completion of this particular preregistration; it does **not** establish that TSA markets or all calendar-count models are unresearchable or unprofitable.

The useful deliverable is a bounded source/semantics diagnosis and tested reusable accounting, not ten separate discoveries. There is no economic validation result, demonstrated edge, completed real backtest, or qualifying score claim. Mechanical no-order cash retention must not be described as a zero-return strategy backtest.

## Evidence preserved
- 81 archived markets map to 79 fixed target-date slots. The first 39 development dates and last 40 holdout dates remain unchanged; conflicting or missing cases were not replaced.
- The metadata selector finds three strike conflicts and one target-date conflict. All 79 slots additionally lack the specific timezone proof required by this preregistration. These counts overlap, so they are not 83 distinct exclusions.
- All 81 final count labels parse. Duplicate Sep09 arms have the same count, not contradictory count observations. Aug22's date-semantic conflict excludes that label as a feature source. Settlement timestamps plus a seven-day lag are an availability proxy, not proof of the original TSA publication vintage or historical API contents.
- The official TSA contract PDF says data are published **by** next-day 9am ET. That is not a lower bound on earliest publication; merely trading before 9am does not prove an unknown-outcome decision. The PDF defines release/trading clocks, not the statistical day's timezone. Its 2026 retrieval/last-modified record also is not proof that these exact bytes were the 2021 contemporaneous rules.
- The 40 holdout dates span ten natural weeks. Shared lagged observations and travel seasonality can correlate errors; neither 40 distinct dates nor ten reporting blocks proves statistical independence.
- New trade requests were 0/99. Missing price, count, and volume remain null, not observed zero. One previously inspected 69-trade feasibility tape stays separately disclosed and is not new validation or a replacement for missing eligible windows.
- The probability implementation was frozen before holdout tape analysis. Earlier raw schema output exposed archived result/price fields, and one feasibility tape had already been seen, so the project does not claim full blindness.
- Fees, settlement locks, integer quantities, and capital arithmetic were tested synthetically. Historical 2021 fee versions and own executable quotes/fills remain unverified. Future-window volume is ex-post conditional capacity, not a live input or guaranteed own liquidity.
