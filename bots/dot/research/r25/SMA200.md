## Frozen definition and capital convention

For UTC day t, exposure is 1 only when BTC close(t−1) is strictly greater than the arithmetic mean of closes t−200 through t−1; otherwise exposure is 0. The daily gross reference return is exposure × [close(t)/close(t−1) − 1]. The current day's close is not used to determine its exposure. Equality means flat. The 200-day parameter and BTC asset are unchanged from r20/r24, without parameter fitting, ETH substitution, asset selection or threshold selection.

The source is the existing Binance spot history with 2023 warmup, overlaid by frozen r20/r24 Binance spot_close values for 2024 onward, precisely as r24 did. Kraken prices are used in the R25 strategy but deliberately do not replace this frozen Binance BTC benchmark. January 1, 2024 includes its December 31-to-January 1 close return; there are 1,004 daily return observations through September and 1,023 through October, rather than dropping January 1.

Benchmark NAV compounds those daily gross returns from initial 5,000. When long, all current benchmark equity receives the BTC return; when flat it earns zero. This is an unlevered binary long/cash gross reference, not fixed-q matching to R25. It has zero transaction fees, slippage, financing, interest, custody and tax by definition, including signal switches and terminal valuation. No executable signal-time fills are asserted. The same-capital comparison is transparent but not risk/exposure matched: the benchmark can deploy all 5,000 directionally, while R25 has two isolated venues and a paired position. Its different reinvestment/exposure convention is intentional and frozen. The original matched-q buyhold and idle-cash comparisons remain separate.

The primary R25 strategy starts with 5,000 and daily returns equal current NAV / prior NAV − 1, with first denominator 5,000. This differs intentionally from the original report's additional NAV-change-versus-market diagnostic: the supplement restores the exact frozen normalized-return correlation convention. For the 2,500 sensitivity, remove 2,500 idle capital from every strategy NAV and initialize the return series at 2,500; it is not a claim that the dual-venue strategy can operate with only 2,500. The gross reference is initialized at the same stated benchmark capital. Primary capital remains 5,000 everywhere.

## Endpoint and calendar alignment

September standalone replaces only September 30's marked NAV with its fee-adjusted spot/perp liquidation value. October continuation carries the January position through September without liquidation and replaces only October 19 with its liquidation value. All entry costs remain charged once. Daily strategy returns include these endpoint adjustments; the gross benchmark has no fees to adjust.

Monthly observations are compounded daily returns. Exactly January 2024 through September 2026, 33 complete UTC months, enter monthly correlation for both views. September's strategy monthly return can differ between views because only the standalone view charges its hypothetical September exit. The October partial block is retained separately, with 19 days, and is never a 34th complete-month correlation observation. Its strategy return starts at unliquidated September marked NAV, so it differs from the change between separately liquidated endpoint PnLs. No October run-rate annualization is introduced.
